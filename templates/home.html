Adds the /gif command to your team's Slack for all public and private channels:

  • All Public Channels: Allows you to preview your GIF before sending it to the channel.
  • Direct/Group Message Channels: No preview is available, please GIF responsibly.

This app will modify your Slack in the following way:

  • New Slash Command: New slash command /gif will be added for searching GIF's.
  • Add BOT User: A Bot user @slashgif will be used when posting GIF's to public channels.

Inspired and powered by Giphy, SlashGIF allows you to post GIF's to public channels in Slack, without accidentally posting an offensive image. Many people have begun using Giphy, but due to the random nature of searches combined with crowd sourced tags, this can produce undesired results in enterprise environments. With SlashGIF, you never have to worry about unintentionally posting questionable or NSFW GIF's in public channels again.
